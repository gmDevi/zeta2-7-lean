## Main theorem (`Zeta2Lean/Z7/Main.lean`)

```lean
theorem Zeta2.Z7.zeta2_7_irrational :
    (∀ s : ℕ, HasVolkenborn (halfPow s) (J s)) ∧ ¬ ∃ q : ℚ, zeta2 7 = q
```

with the trusted definitions of `Zeta2Lean/Defs.lean` (`zeta2 7 = J 6 / 768`, `J s` the Volkenborn

## The construction (`Z7/Defs.lean`)

Everything is indexed by `u : ℕ` with `n = 40u` (no division by 40 anywhere).

    η = (5, 7, 7, 9, 11, 13)/40,   C_m = [η_m n, (1-η_m) n - 1]              (eta, block)
    a_k = 1, 2, 3, 4 for min(k, n-k) < 2u, < 4u, < 6u, else                   (apole)
    R_n(t) = (2t + n) ∏_m ∏_{c ∈ C_m} (t + 1/2 + c) / ∏_{k ≤ n} (t + k)^{a_k}
    G_k(ε) = ε^{a_k} R_n(-k+ε),   r_{i,k} = [ε^{a_k - i}] G_k                  (Gser, rcoef)
    S_n := -∫ R_n'''(t + 1/2 - n/2) dt = ρ + 60 c₃ J₆ = ρ + Z ζ₂(7),  Z = 46080 c₃   (rho, Zc)

* `N = 136u = 3.4n`, `∑ a_k = N + 4`, `deg R_n = -3`, `R_n(-n-t) = -R_n(t)`.
* **Sign convention**: the Lean `S_n` is the *negative* of proof §2's (the pair's convention:
  integrand `-R'''`), so `rho u = -ρ_n` and `Zc u = -Z_n`. Valuations and sizes are unchanged.
  `rhoP u = -rho u` is the proof's `ρ_n` in the `∑_k sgn(d_k) ∑_{h ∈ H_k} X(k,h)` form.
* **Centring**: `integrand u x = ∑_{i,k} (i)₃ r_{i,k} (x + k - 20u + 1/2)^{-(i+3)}` has pole
  positions of both signs; `AhalfZ d s` is the centred translation correction
  (`∫ (t + 1/2 + d)^{-s} = J_s - s AhalfZ d (s+1)` for every `d ∈ ℤ`), `halfPowZ` the integer
  version of `halfPow`.
* **Local models** (proof §4.2): `localModel u q k₀ : LocalType` (doubled local coordinates
  `w = 2v`; items `(w, e)`: poles, zeros, the `δ`-zero; bad pairs `(w_k, w_e, sgn)`), with `E`,
  `netE`, `rSer` (= `r_ξ(y)`), `ASer` (= `A_ξ(1/y)`, so `[y^p](y^E A) = [y^{p-E}] ASer`), `coeffD` (`[y^p] D_{ξ,κ}` for
  `κ = c₀ + c y^j`), `LevGe` (level `≥ P - 5`), `taylorAt`, `PoleLevAt`, `PoleLevGe`
  (Lemma 4.7). Class sums: `classBad` (`β_ξ`), `goodPart` (`G`), `kerRes` (`∮_{D_ξ} R K`),
  `farRes` (`∮_{D_ξ} R g_{j,ξ}`), `Xk`, `oddBetween`, `negChoose`. All transcribed literally in
  `python/z7_mirror.py` and checked against the research engines.
* **Denominators**: `ValGe q L x` (`v_q(x) ≥ L` via `padicNorm`), `eq7 u q` (exponent of `q` in
  the lcm of the denominators of `ρ, Z`).
* **Table** (`Z7/DenTable.lean`, generated by `python/z7_den_table.py`): `DenRow = [a, b) × (f,
  κ = c₀ + c y^j)`; `headRows7` (469 kernel-level rows on `[1, 20)`), `resRows7` (980
  residue-level rows on `[20, 60)`), `denTable7` = head ++ res ++ res+40 ++ res+80 (`[1, 140)`).
  Kernel-checked facts in the same file: `denTable7_sane` (row side conditions) and
  `denTable7_integral_fixed` (`∑ f (1/a - 1/b) + 10/140 ≤ 3.99` in fixed point; exact `ℚ`
  arithmetic does not reduce in the kernel).
* **Growth**: `Fx`, `upotTerms` (18 terms), `Upot` = `U(κ)` of proof §5.
* **Rates**: `g7 = 0.71 ≥ U* = 0.70670223`, `delta7 = 3.99 ≥ E = 3.9240892` (the "Lean-friendly
  truncation" of proof §0.2/§10), `g7 + delta7 = 4.70 < 6.8 log 2 = 4.71340` (`margin7`).

## The assembly (`Z7/Assembly.lean`, proved)

Suppose `ζ₂(7) = r ∈ ℚ` (proof §8).
1. `Stmt_Denominators7`: eventually `D u` is odd and `2^{e u} D ρ`, `2^{e u} D Z` are integers;
   `a₀ = num(2^e D ρ)`, `a₁ = num(2^e D Z)` (no choice).
2. `L_u = a₀ + a₁ ζ₂(7) = 2^e D S_n` (`Stmt_L7`, `linear_form_zeta7`), `B_u = |a₀| + |a₁|`.
3. `L_u ≠ 0` frequently (`Stmt_Nonvanishing7`).
4. `‖2^e D‖₂ = 2^{-e}` (`norm_two_pow_mul_odd`), so `B_u ‖L_u‖₂ = D(|ρ| + |Z|)‖S_n‖₂ ≤
   2c(u+1)^A e^{-(6.8 log 2 - g - δ - 2ε) 40u}` (`bound_core7`) `→ 0` with `ε = (6.8 log 2 - g - δ)/4`.
5. Lai's criterion with one constant (`Stmt_Criterion`, `k = 1`) gives the contradiction.

## The denominator bound: design

**Semantic interface.** Theorem A only sees `Stmt_ExpBounds7`, five prime-by-prime bounds on
`eq7 u q`: `big` (`q > n`: 0), `small` (every odd `q`: `19 ⌊log_q(2n+1)⌋`), `crude`
(`q ≥ 29`, `q² > 2n + 3q`: 14), `tail` (`q > 1000`, `q ∤ u`, `n/q ≥ 20`: 10) and `rows`
(`q > 1000`, `q ∤ u`, `n/q ∈ [a, b)` of a table row: `f`). The analytic part
(`Denominators7`) then follows the pair's `log_sum_bound`: `small` for `q ≤ max(1000, √(3n))`,
`crude` for `q ∣ u` (total `≤ 14 log u`), `tail` for `n/q ≥ 140` (`≤ 10 θ(n/140)`), `rows` on
`[1, 140)` (PNT on 3409 intervals), and nothing above `n`. Constant:
`∫_1^20 f dx/x² + ∫_20^140 f⁰ dx/x² + 10/140 = 3.5868669525 + 0.2657936371 + 0.0714285714 = 3.9240891610 < 3.99`.

**Theorem K interface.** `Stmt_ThmK7` turns a *certificate for all classes* (a kernel
`κ = c₀ + c y^j` with `LevGe … (T+5)` and `PoleLevGe … T` for every class, `T ≤ 0`, far cost
`j - 4 ≥ T`) into `e_q ≤ -T`. It is proved from the four lemmas of proof §4.3–4.7
(`ClassLemma7`, `KernelIdentity7`, `FarPart7`, `PerPole7`) plus `Emin7`, and the elementary
splitting `rho = -rhoP = -(∑_ξ classBad + goodPart)`. The trivial kernel `κ = 0` with
`T = E_min - 4` is the residue level, so `crude` and `tail` are also instances of `ThmK7`.

**Certificates.** `HeadCert7` (kernel level on `[1, 20)`) and `ResCert7` (residue level on one
period) state *what* the certificates give (the hypotheses of `ThmK7` for every class at every
admissible prime), not *how* they are encoded, so the certificate team is free to choose the
representation. Numbers from the research data: 469 head rows, **1112 distinct continuum types**
(`≤ 35` items, `≤ 100` pairs), 9380 (row, type) incidences; kernels `κ = 0` (336 rows), `κ = 1`
(28), constant `κ = 1 + c` (99; 10 distinct `c`, denominators 17-smooth, up to 56 digits),
`κ = 1 - (5/4) y²` (6 rows on `[2, 20/9)`, the only use of the far part). Recommended encoding:
the pair's `certOK_sound` technique (integer parts of the ~20 event lines at the midpoint + the
order of their fractional parts; `Vl`-style floor sums), a global table of the distinct types
with their levels checked once by `decide +kernel` on computable (`List`, `ℚ`) versions of
`coeffD`/`taylorAt`, and per-row type-index lists. Only coefficients up to degree `≤ 15` are ever
needed (`P ≤ 5`, `E ≥ -10`). The residue part is a direct adaptation of the pair's 766 `CC`
certificates (`E ∈ [-6, 6]` on the period).

**Fallbacks (margin bookkeeping, with `g7 = 0.71`).** Break-even for `delta7` is
`6.8 log 2 - 0.71 = 4.0034`. Without the far part (the six `[2, 20/9)` rows get `f + 1`,
kernel `κ = 1`): `E = 3.9741`. With the residue level only up to 100 instead of 140:
`E = 3.9352`. Both stay below `delta7 = 3.99`; the table generator makes such changes mechanical
(and `delta7` can be raised to `4.00` if `g7` stays `0.71`, since `4.71 < 4.7134`).

## Mathematical notes (architect's review)

* Every definition and statement was checked numerically against the research engines (0 failures,
  `python/z7_mirror_full.log`). The class lemma formula with the explicit residues (`kerRes`,
  `farRes`) — in particular the residue at the kernel pole `-k₀`, which involves *all* poles of
  `R_n` through its partial fractions — is not written out in the research proof; it was derived
  here and verified exactly (16600 class tests at `n = 200`).
* The research code's kernel step (`levels.kernel_step_bound`) uses the `κ = 1` level for types
  with `E + 1 > j`; this is harmless because such types have `Lev_κ ≥ E - 4 ≥ P₀ + 1` and the
  step is capped at `P₀ + 1` (proof Theorem 4.6); in the Lean form (`LevGe … (5 - f)`) the range
  of indices is then empty.
* `PoleLevGe` (the refined per-pole bound) is never binding on `[1, 20)` (`LG ≥ -f` everywhere in
  the data), consistent with proof §4.9.
* No gap was found. The points needing the most care in Lean are the continuum/discrete dictionary
  (boundary conventions of `localModel`: pole existence `k₀ + jq ≤ n` inclusive, cells
  `min(k, n-k) < 2u, 4u, 6u`, zeros strict, exposure strict) and the q-adic local expansion of the
  class lemma.
